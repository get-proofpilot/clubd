What Makes Them Special

  • Frictionless UX: Text-based invites, no app download required, one-tap RSVP. Google's Best App of 2024.
  • "Who's Going" Transparency: Seeing friends on the guest list converts undecided people. The social snowball effect.
  • Gen Z Aesthetic: Meme-based designs, GIFs, humor — event planning feels fun, not utilitarian.
  • "Crush" Feature (2026): Anonymously signal romantic interest in people from events you've both attended. Dating layered onto social overlap.
  • Expanding Beyond Parties: a16z mandated Partiful for all NY Tech Week events — 1,000+ person professional conferences.

Why Partiful Won't Compete With Clubd

Partiful is invite-only. You create an event and invite specific people. There is no public discovery — no "what's happening near me" feed. They're built for private parties and personal events, not community event discovery.

What Clubd Should Steal

  • The "who's going" social proof should be Clubd's most prominent UI element
  • Frictionless guest experience — no download required for first touch
  • Personality-driven design — playful, not corporate
  • Consider a "Crush" / matchmaking layer for community events down the line

10 — Ideal Customer Profiles

Host ICPs

Three host profiles that drive Clubd's supply side — and revenue

🧘

The Fitness Instructor

Pilates studio, yoga teacher, run club leader, boot camp coach

The Pain: "I spend more time on marketing than teaching."

Current Stack: Instagram + Linktree + Google Forms + Mailchimp + Canva + Venmo

Why Clubd: Replaces 6 tools with one. Events auto-discoverable. Payment processing built in.

Revenue: Clubd Pro ($29-99/mo) + Booking commission + Promoted events

🤝

The Community Organizer

Dad walk group, book club, beach cleanup, nonprofit, social league

The Pain: "I organize events for free and can barely get the word out."

Current Stack: Facebook group or group text + maybe Instagram

Why Clubd: Free to list. Discovery feed brings new people organically. Social graph brings friends-of-friends.

Revenue: Free → Clubd Pro as group grows → Marketing services for big events

🍺

The Local Business Host

Brewery, restaurant, coffee shop, retail store hosting events

The Pain: "We host events for foot traffic but only regulars know about them."

Current Stack: Instagram + in-store flyers + maybe Eventbrite

Why Clubd: Reaches event-seeking consumers not already following them. Analytics on conversion.

Revenue: Promoted events ($50-500) + Commission + Venue partnership ($199-999/mo)

From Sweatpals

Sweatpals built waiver signing because fitness hosts need it. Clubd should identify vertical-specific tools: waivers for fitness, dietary forms for food events, volunteer tracking for nonprofits.

Expansion

Study which cities worked and why. Miami, Austin, Chicago, NYC, LA all have distinct community event cultures. OC is the perfect proving ground.

From Partiful

Design

12 — Strategic Recommendations

Five Moves to
Win the Market

01

Own "Lifestyle Events" — Not Everything

Yes: Run clubs, yoga, popup dinners, brewery events, beach cleanups, art walks, dad groups, matcha meetups. No: Tech meetups, corporate networking, gaming events, political rallies.

02

Lead with Social, Not Search

Home screen leads with "Your friends are going to…" and "People like you attended…" — not a category grid. This is THE differentiator vs. every competitor.

03

Start Hyper-Local in OC

Launch with 30-50 handpicked hosts across all lifestyle categories. Prove the social flywheel in one market. OC's demo is the perfect testbed.

04

Build the Media Arm Immediately

Launch "Clubd OC" Instagram/TikTok now — even before the app. Curate "things to do this weekend" content. Build audience → launch → funnel. Steal Fever's playbook at city scale.

05

Make the Free Tier Unbeatable for Hosts

Sweatpals and Fever charge hosts. Clubd should make the free tier generous enough that every run club leader, yoga instructor, and community organizer lists without hesitation. Monetize through Pro tools, commissions, and promotions — not by gatekeeping basic event listing.
