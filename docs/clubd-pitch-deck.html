07 — Business Model

Free for users. Five
revenue engines.

📣

Promoted Events

Hosts and venues pay to boost visibility. Category sponsorships, featured placements, and targeted recommendations.

$50–500/event

Target: 30% of revenue

👑

Clubd Pro (Booking Software)

Full event booking platform: ticketing, payment processing, attendee management, recurring events, analytics, CRM, and co-host tools.

$29–99/mo

Target: 25% of revenue

💳

Booking Commission

Built-in booking engine for paid events. Clubd processes payments and takes a commission on every ticket sold through the platform.

5–8% per transaction

Target: 20% of revenue

🎨

Marketing Services

On-platform marketing team: professional event graphics, social media campaigns, and paid ad management to help hosts promote and fill events.

$99–499/campaign

Target: 15% of revenue

🏢

Venue Partnerships

Verified venue profiles with booking integrations. Referral revenue from venues filling off-peak hours with community events.

$199–999/mo

Target: 10% of revenue

Users never pay. Revenue comes from the supply side — hosts, bookings, and brands.
